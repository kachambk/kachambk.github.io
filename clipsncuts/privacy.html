ClipsNCuts Privacy Policy

Effective August 19, 2026 · Last updated August 19, 2026

ClipsNCuts (“the app,” “we,” “us”) helps you find nearby Great Clips haircut deals from a ZIP code. This policy describes what information the app handles and why. ClipsNCuts is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Great Clips, Inc.

Information we handle

We do not require you to create an account or provide your name or email.

  • ZIP code and search radius. You type a ZIP, or you can optionally allow location so we can fill the ZIP once. We store the ZIP and radius on your device so the app can show nearby deals.
  • Location (optional). Location is requested only when you tap “Use my location,” never on launch. Coordinates are used once to look up a ZIP (via the device geocoder, and if needed a reverse-geocode request to BigDataCloud). We do not store GPS coordinates. Only the resulting ZIP is kept on your device.
  • Saved deals and family calendar. Offers you save, family member nicknames, and haircut reminder dates stay on your device.
  • Anonymous device ID. A random ID is created on your device so “Worked?” confirmations and deal-alert settings can be associated with this install, not with your identity.
  • Deal alerts (optional). If you turn on alerts, we store your device ID, ZIP, search radius, platform (iOS/Android), and an Expo push token so we can notify you about new deals near that ZIP. You can turn alerts off in the app.
  • Offer confirmations (optional). If you tap whether a deal worked, we store that vote with the anonymous device ID so other users can see community counts.
  • Subscriptions. If you start a trial or subscribe, Apple, Google, and RevenueCat process the purchase. We receive entitlement status (whether the install is premium), not your full payment card details.

Where that information goes

  • On your device: ZIP, saved deals, family calendar, alert preference, anonymous device ID.
  • Firebase (Google): deal-alert subscriptions and offer confirmation votes, keyed by anonymous device ID.
  • Expo: push delivery when deal alerts are enabled.
  • RevenueCat, Apple, and Google: subscription purchase and restore.
  • GitHub: the public offers catalog the app downloads.
  • Great Clips / Sparkfly: Redeem opens their official offer page. Their privacy policy applies on that site.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use the data above for advertising profiles.

Analytics

The current production app does not send usage analytics to a third-party analytics vendor. Debug builds may log events locally on the developer’s machine.

Children

ClipsNCuts is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Retention and your choices

On-device data is removed if you delete the app. Turning off deal alerts removes the remote alert subscription. You can decline location permission and still use the app by entering a ZIP yourself.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
